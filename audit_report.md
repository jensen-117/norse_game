## The quarry: primers for words the language still needs

- **Bound:** Only the Drattr's net. Wanted: a hero's net or snare, 'caught, lose a turn', rarer than a stun.
- **Chilled:** No hero carries cold yet. The Hafrunar's word: 'the cold gets into their joints', then a second chill locks them (Frozen).
- **Cover:** Foes cover only by the Beinbryn's family rule, not as a move. Fine as is; a bodyguard boss could say it out loud.
- **Cursed:** No hero curses yet. The Seidar's word (Dokkn, planned): 'ill luck that rots their defences'.
- **Death:** Helvad lives on a staff and one spirit. Rare by design: a hero version is a relic's job, not a class's.
- **Disarmed:** No hero disarms (Mogl is foes-only, and rarely). Wanted: 'knock the weapon away', a duelist's or a wrestler's word.
- **Feared:** No hero frightens yet (Ogn is foes-only). Wanted: 'they flinch, freeze up and drop their guard'. Also Ogn's planned job on the element ladder.
- **Feeble:** No foe weakens a hero's arm. A curse-thrower or a venomous bite: 'your grip fails'.
- **Frozen:** Reached only by chilling twice. Wanted: a hero who can set up and cash in the freeze, and a word for 'shatter it' as a choice.
- **Keen:** Steady the eye: 'I see where it's weak.' The answer to Blinded, and the archer's or the duelist's self-buff (Aukhitt is the plain build).
- **Knot:** Only the Skurmar stores turns. Fine: it's his identity.
- **MAT down:** No word yet (Waning, planned): 'their magic thins'. A Seidar or Dagskald answer to casters.
- **MDF down:** No word yet (Hexed, planned): 'magic sinks into them'. The caster party's Sundered.
- **MDF up:** Only foes ward (Allaukstav). Wanted for heroes (Warded, planned): 'the stave holds the spell off'.
- **Marked:** No foe marks. A spotter foe ('that one!') would make Evasive and Cover answers.
- **Pinned:** No foe pins. A hunter foe that nails a hero in place would make Move and Skrida matter.
- **Revive:** No foe raises the fallen. A necromancer ('get up') is the natural Bein or Draugr caster.
- **Scorched:** No foe burns heroes. An Eld foe that leaves a fire would make Skolja worth a slot.
- **Shove:** No foe shoves. A charging beast that knocks a hero into the back row would make the board breathe.
- **Silenced:** Only the Hunt silences, and it isn't in any room yet. A foe caster-hunter would test the Dagskald.
- **Slowed:** No hero slows yet. Wanted: 'drag at their feet', a Hafrunar or Lundar word (roots, mud, cold water).
- **Song:** No foe sings. A war-chanter that heals its pack each round would be a priority kill.
- **Taunt:** No foe taunts. A boss or a shield-bearer that says 'look at me' would make the party choose targets.
