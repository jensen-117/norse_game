# Loop Spec v2

> **Nov 20: largely set aside by pillars.md.** The game is now a fight-run with fixed characters (a party of three classes, no levelling), not a city of hires. The city economy (the licence on credit, wages, debt, the Register, the seven-tick day) is parked, not deleted. **The Company stays as the meta hub** (unlocks, talismans, the hall of tapestries). Part 8 (the Gate and the dive) is superseded by dive_spec.md. Contracts, rumours and the runes remain useful as unlock and event material.

The build-to for everything around the fights: the city, the day, recruitment, runes, contracts, the Gate, money, and storage. It sits beside **combat_spec.md** (the fights) and **bestiary.md** (what's in them). Part 16 of the combat spec (rooms, the map, the Rune Gate) still holds; this spec wraps a city and a calendar around it.

**Status key.** **Locked** means agreed in alignment (Nov 7–8; open items settled Nov 8). **Proposed** means a default chosen to make the spec buildable; it holds until play says otherwise. Numbers marked *(tune)* are starting values.

## Part 1: The Premise (Locked)

- **A city long past the sagas.** Norse-tinted medieval, with a strong touch of English bureaucracy: guilds, merchants, boards and governing bodies, permits and stamps. Magic is woven into everyday life.
- **The Rune Gate** is the one wild thing left. Spoken to with three runes, it opens onto another place, and possibly another time: monsters and treasure.
- **Divers** make their living through it: diving for rare things to sell, hunting named monsters, taking contracts from the guilds and merchants. **You are a new diver.**
- **The tone is the bureaucracy.** A Diver's licence on credit, a toll at the Gate, stamped contracts, a Register of hirelings, a collector who comes on the seventh day, and a licence card with a stamp for every payment.

---

## Part 2: The Start (Locked)

1. **An intro.** The city, the Gate, what divers do.
2. **Choose a class** for your diver (the main character).
3. **Land in the city** with nothing. The Board of the Gate issues your **Diver's licence on credit: 20,000 marks**, at **400 a week**, the first payment due a week after signing (Part 9). Signing is a no-brainer, and that's the joke. Once you've signed, the **Diver's Supply** will sell to you.
4. **Find some other weaklings, knock out a few dungeons, pay the bills,** and get on with your life.

## Part 3: The Day (Locked shape; ticks tune)

- **Seven ticks:** Early Morning, Morning, Noon, Afternoon, Evening, Night, Midnight. **The day turns over at midnight.**
- **Walking the city is free.** **Doing something costs a tick:** listening for rumours, scouting for recruits, Scrying, taking a service.
- **A full dive costs three ticks,** one per leg (Part 8). You can come out after any leg and keep what you found. In practice a full dive starts by Evening, or Midnight comes first (Part 8).
- **Rest heals everything, and it ends your day.** You sleep until the next Early Morning, so the ticks you had left are gone. Rest at Night or Midnight and you lose almost nothing; rest at Early Morning and you've taken the whole day off. Ending the day by or before Midnight, then resting, is the game's rhythm.
- **The budget a day was sized for:** two recruits plus one for safety (three ticks) and a full dive (three), with one tick to spare.

## Part 5: Recruitment and the Register (Locked)

- **Your party is you plus two hirelings** (three in a fight). You are always in it. *(Kept open: one third of the party never changing may chafe.)*
- **The Register starts full enough to play:** one hireling of each class you didn't choose (eleven), rolled at the start. You can always field a party by Scrying.
- **Each day, two people around the city will join you** (a tick to Scout, at the Inn, the Tavern, or by Waiting in the Square). They're new heroes a little behind you. Early on they ramp up with you; from your level 6 they roll 2–3 levels behind *(a data table)*:

| Your level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recruits roll | 1 | 1–2 | 1–3 | 2–3 | 3 | 3–4 | yours −3 to −2 |
 Scout shows the crowd: "After browsing the crowd and chatting to a few people, these ones stand out..." *(Later: a recruitment dialogue minigame.)*
- **Everyone you recruit goes on the Register** as well as into your party. Anyone on the Register can be **Scryed:** entering Scry costs one tick, then you summon as many as you like, each paid their day's wage up front (which covers that day's dive). **Dismiss** sends a hireling back to the Register.
- **Hirelings grow only with you.** Each has a card you'll look at often; they level by diving at your side. A hero who is Down when a fight ends gets no XP for it. XP thins out against enemies below a hero's level, so a hireling who trails you earns more from the same fights and catches up, but never overtakes you.
- **Wages are 40 marks a dive,** paid on return to the city *(tune)*. Hirelings who were Down are still paid; they came. A hireling's rate is **their value, shifted by personality and by their liking for you**, so favourites cost less and some people are simply greedy.
- **Death in the slice is never final for long:** a dead hireling is away recovering for a week (Part 8). Later, the dead may leave the Register.

## Part 6: Runes and Rumours (Locked)

- **You can only speak runes you know.** Known runes live in your **Codex**.
- **You learn runes from people:** Listen and Mingle at the Inn, the Bar, the Square. Some tell you the reliable rune words they use for specific things.
- **The specials of the day.** Each day, two people (the Innkeeper among them) name a rune combo with a modifier on it: heavier enemies, more treasure, an extra boss. **A special is open only on the day it's named.** Each names its modifier, so choosing between them is a real choice.
- **Same words, same place** (combat spec Part 16): a triple always opens the same dungeon, so words can be shared. **Known runes combine freely;** invented words open only their exact authored phrase.
- **Rumour seeds** (later): an authored triple advertised for a few days, with something special behind it.

**The slice's runes** (all built on the Barrow's art; place runes change which element visits, and the Draugr and Beinar are always there). You start knowing **Yr, Fe and Tyr**, one full triple, and learn the rest by Listening.

| Rune | Kind | In the slice |
|---|---|---|
| Yr (yew) | place | the Barrow, with Natt and Aska guests and Beinseidr |
| Kaun (torch) | place | the Barrow, with Eld guests |
| Is (ice) | place | the Barrow, with Hrim guests |
| Fe (wealth) | rooms | the chance rooms lean to treasure |
| Ar (harvest) | rooms | the chance rooms lean to rest |
| Tyr (war) | threat | the chance rooms lean to fights |
| Thurs (giant) | threat | packs roll bigger ranks |
| Naud (need) | threat | the Hunt is faster, and pays more |

**The specials' modifiers** (one per special): **Heavy** (packs a budget step bigger), **Gilded** (gold ×1.5), **Crowned** (an extra Drott at the last elite), **Bountied** (elites drop boss loot), **Quiet** (the Hunt slower), **Rich** (more trash). Ur gear comes later.

## Part 7: Contracts and Arcs (Locked direction; later than the barest slice)

- **Proprietors post contracts:** the Apothecary wants Eldberries for fire potions, the Armory wants bog iron.
- **Bread-and-butter contracts are doable in one dive.** A contract comes with its place: "I heard **Vast Overcast Heatcave** is ripe with Eldberries; can you bring me at least 20?" Speak that triple and its events lean toward Eldberry bushes and its packs drop more of them.
- **More than asked pays a bonus.** Fewer can be turned in until the deadline *(Proposed: a few days)*.
- **Arcs, for the demo** (written once everything runs):
  - **The Trainer's arc:** loosely narrative, tutorial-like quests that pay well. Each asks you to do something in a fight (land a Weak hit, Cover an ally, use a Bid move, scatter a bone pile, flee with a Smoke Pot), so the mechanics are put on display. They're tied to their own seeds, and the whole arc pays about **2,000 marks** *(tune)*. The engine's event stream makes these objectives cheap: each is a check on the fight's events.
  - **The challenge arc:** about five curated seeds ending in **named packs**, such as the Sergeant's, the Captain's and the General's.
  - Together they answer "all the tutorials are done, now what?" and carry the slice to a demo.

## Part 8: The Gate and the Dive (Locked shape; numbers tune)

- **Speak three runes you know, pay the toll** *(tune)*. The Gate's clerk warns you: **your hirelings work until Midnight.**
- **The dungeon is three legs of five rooms** (each rune governs its own leg; combat spec Part 16): **a fight on the way in, three rooms of chance** (fight, event, treasure, rest), and **an elite on the way out.** So a dive has at least six fights and usually about ten.
- **Each leg costs one tick.** You can leave after any leg and keep the loot.
- **Rest rooms offer one of three choices:** **Rest** (recover HP and MP, and raise the Down), **Organize** (rearrange pouches from the Haul), or **Carve Runes** (+5% to one stat for the rest of the dive *(tune; watch it)*). Carving stacks: a dive that skips every rest can end with a big bonus, and that's a choice to watch in play.

| Rune | Meaning | Carved, it gives |
|---|---|---|
| Tyr | the war god | ATK |
| Oss | the god of speech and magic | MAT |
| Thurs | the giant | DEF |
| Bjarkan | the birch, shelter | MDF |
| Ur | the aurochs | max HP |
| Ar | the harvest | max MP |
| Reid | the ride | SPD |
| Sol | the sun, sight | ACC |
| Logr | water, flowing | EVA |

- **Midnight negotiation.** If a dive rolls over to Midnight, your hirelings stop you, one after the other, each asking another day's wage (40 *(tune)*, shifted by personality and liking for you). Pay in coin, or barter loot from the Haul of equal or greater value (Goblin Ears at 4 marks each toward 40); no change is given, so paying 44 gets you nothing extra. If you can't or won't pay, they leave, and you're alone: push on, or use a Heim Rune.
- **The Hunt clock** runs as in the combat spec; when it catches you, the Hunt (the Oskorei) rides in. It keeps its pace past Midnight.
- **Dying, Wounded, Dead** (combat spec Part 10). A hero at 0 HP is Dying, with five breaths; a Surgeon's Kit or Halvakna brings them back, and winning the fight stabilises them at 1 HP, Wounded. The Dead need the Mead of Waking or Mervakna. Rest clears Wounded; it doesn't raise the Dead.
- **Coming home dead.** If your main character dies and the hirelings carry you home, the **City Infirmary** stabilises you and charges for it *(tune: a modest fee)*: you're back at full HP, Wounded. A hireling who comes home dead is revived off-screen, leaves your party, and is away recovering for a week; scout a replacement.
- **A party wipe:** you lose the dive's loot, hirelings go unpaid, and the Board's wardens carry everyone home. The Infirmary charges for you; the dead hirelings are away a week, the Dying ones come home Wounded.
- **If you can't pay the Infirmary,** the charge goes on your loan. A licence needs a living holder.

## Part 9: Money (Locked shape; numbers tune)

- **The currency is the mark** (a Norse weight of silver; plain English too).
- **The anchors:**

| Thing | Marks *(tune)* |
|---|---|
| A regular fight | 25 (half a Life Draught), plus about two pieces of trash |
| An elite | 50, plus about three pieces of trash |
| A piece of trash (Draugr Fang, Goblin Ear) | 2–8 |
| Bespoke boss loot | hundreds |
| Small Life Draught / Life Draught / Strong Life Draught | 20 / 50 / 100 |
| The worst weapon in the game (grade H) | 100; each grade up about 50% more (150 at G); good finds 25–100 over |
| Lodging | 30 a night |
| The Gate toll | 25 a dive at grade H, rising with grade (100 at A) |
| A hireling's wage | 40 a dive |
| Mead of Waking | about 150 *(tune)* |

- **What a dive is worth:** about 300 for a lean one (six fights), about 440 for a likely one (ten). Less lodging, the toll, two wages and a draught or two, a likely dive day nets about 200. A week of four dives and three prep days nets about **700**.
- **Collection day comes every seventh day.** The Board's collector takes the **400-mark installment** and the Inn takes the week's lodging. You see both bills coming all week, and your licence card gets a stamp for each payment.
- **A missed installment** adds a late fee of 10% (40 marks). **Two missed** suspends your licence: the Gate won't answer you until you pay.
- **Paying early is allowed.** The loan runs about a year; nobody is expected to finish it in the demo.
- **Grade and level are two ladders.** Grade (H, G, F ... A, S, SS, SSS: eleven) is the licence and guild rank: it sets weapon tiers and opens deeper runes, through a licensing exam. Level is the character. XP thins out against enemies below your grade, so you can grind grade H forever, but you won't want to. **Everyone is grade H in the slice, so weapon upgrades sit outside it.**

## Part 10: Storage (Locked)

- **The Pouch:** every hero has their own, with **3 slots**. It's the only thing usable in a fight; a hero uses their own pouch with their own action. Configured in town, and in a dive only at a rest room (Organize).
- **Stacks shrink as items get stronger:** Small draughts 3, plain draughts 2, Strong draughts 1 (Life and Spirit alike); Pocket Sand, Firecrackers, throwables and Smoke Pots 3.
- **The Haul:** the party's storage, not anyone's pockets. **Consumables** (spares, handed out into pouches before a dive) and **Loot** (sold from here).
- **The Quest Ledger:** under the Haul, a short list of your contracts, each with its item count beneath it, grey until done and white when you can turn it in: "Goblin Ears for the Apothecary", then "Goblin Ears ×3/4". It reads from the Loot; the full Journal (runes to use, who to return to, a line of lore) comes later.

## Part 11: Items (Locked names; numbers tune)

Items are **English with Norse flavour** (spellbook, Three Registers). An item is a move with a fixed power rather than a stat, so anyone can use one: a caster can throw an axe properly. Throwables are priced as a patch, not a plan.

| Item | Does | In the slice |
|---|---|---|
| **Small Life Draught, Life Draught, Strong Life Draught** | Restore HP | Small and plain; Strong as a find |
| **Spirit Draught** (Small, plain, Strong) | Restore MP | Yes |
| **Surgeon's Kit** | Bring back a Dying hero at 1 HP, no Wound (as Halvakna); stacks to 3; about 60 | Yes |
| **Mead of Waking** | Raise the Dead at 1 HP, Wounded (as Mervakna); stacks to 1; about 150 | Yes |
| **Antidote, Bandage** | Cure Poisoned, Bleeding (combat spec Part 11) | Later |

## Part 12: The Spine and the Demo (Locked direction)

- **The debt is the spine:** 20,000 marks, 400 a week, about a year. It bites every week; it isn't meant to be finished in the demo. If someone does pay it off, a short dev note thanks them and says there's more to come.
- **What the demo steers by:** the Trainer's arc, then the challenge arc and its named packs (Part 7), on top of regular dives and the specials of the day.
- **Later spines, kept in view:** licence grades and the exam; crafting, fed by contract materials; a story, perhaps seeded by a recurring rumour about the Gate itself.

## Part 13: The Barest Slice (Proposed)

**The build order** (Joseph, Nov 7): the end-to-end fight in the browser; then rooms and the animation around fights; then the **dungeon assembler** (combinatorial rooms, the real test), so the Rune Gate has something to run on; then this city loop; then packaging.

The barest slice, in the browser:

1. **The city:** the Inn, the Tavern, the Town Square, the Diver's Supply, the Gate. Static chatter at each place. Seven ticks a day; Rest ends it.
2. **The Register:** eleven hirelings at the start, Scry and Dismiss, plus two recruits a day around the city. No minigame.
3. **Runes:** the runes you know, plus two specials of the day, each open only that day. No contracts yet.
4. **The Gate:** three legs of five rooms in the Barrow (Draugr, Beinar, Mogel and Ande guests), the eight slice runes, the Hunt clock, rest rooms with Rest, Organize and Carve Runes, the Midnight negotiation. Heroes can be Dying, Wounded, or Dead (combat spec Part 10).
5. **Money:** the licence on credit, collection day (installment and lodging), the toll, wages, gold and trash drops, selling.
6. **Storage:** pouches, the Haul, the licence card.
7. **Growth:** XP and levels for you and your hirelings.
8. **A save** in the browser.

Contracts, the Quest Ledger, the arcs, the full Journal, and Wounded layer on next.

**Engine work for the browser fight is done** (Nov 8): fixed unit ids, items as moves (draughts, throwables, Pocket Sand, Firecracker, Thunderpot, Smoke Pot, Surgeon's Kit, Mead), and Dying, Wounded, Dead with the Vakna ladder.

**Difficulty for dives is its own job:** the sim's families are tuned for comparisons (a random party wins 25–55% of single fights). A dive is about ten fights in a row, so the dungeon assembler sets its own difficulty, roughly 90% for regular fights and 70% for elites.
